Xavier

Xavier's playstyle in Kitty Cards is characterized as defensive, tactical, and gentle. Unlike more aggressive Love Interests (Lls) like Zayne or Sylus, Xavier prefers to hold back, prioritize the player's comfort, and strike with high-value card combos when the moment is right.

Game Setup and Initiative

*   Giving Up Initiative: At the beginning of a match, Xavier will always offer you the choice to go first. In Normal Mode, he will consistently defer to you without resistance.  
*   No Swapping Penalties: If you ask to switch card colors or modify a layout mid-game, he rarely refuses and happily accommodates your wishes.

Advanced Mode Playstyle: The Patient Tactician

When transitioning to Advanced mode, Xavier shifts from purely casual play to a "poker-face" counter-puncher strategy:

*   Combo Counter-Attacking: Xavier rarely acts aggressively early in the round. Instead, he hoards his Assist Cards to set up devastating late-game combos.  
   His primary offensive strategy is stealing cups you have already claimed rather than racing to claim empty ones.  
*   Mind Games & Bluffs: He is notorious for catching players off guard by pretending to have a poor hand.  
   He will intentionally withhold high-tier actions to make you feel safe before flipping the board state.  
*   Strategic "Meow This\!" Saves: Xavier values his Meow This\! (negate) cards highly. He will not waste them on minor plays; he saves them exclusively to protect high-scoring slots or to counter your major aggressive cards like Bye-Bye\! or Purrator.

The "Soft-Hearted" Al Behavior

* Throwing the Game: If you are trailing significantly or about to lose, a prompt may appear signaling your impending defeat. In response, Xavier's Al will often deliberately misplace a kitty or make a suboptimal play to give you a fighting chance.  
* Dragging Out the Match: Instead of delivering a swift finishing blow, he will purposively stretch the game out to spend more time playing with you.

Zayne

Zayne plays a conservative, precise, and calculating style in the Kitty Cards mini-game from Love and Deepspace, frequently hoarding assist and action cards to unleash them all in a sudden board-clearing combo near the end of the match.

Core Playstyle Characteristics

* Assist Card Hoarding: Rather than using special action or assist cards right away, Zayne quietly saves them up throughout the match.  
* Late-Game Ambush: Near the final turns, he drops his accumulated cards back-to-back to flip colors, demolish the player's high-scoring kitties, and steal control of the board.  
*   Cup and Color Control: His primary goal is to maximize his own cup ownership while systematically reversing or deleting the points you have already secured.  
*   High Difficulty: Players frequently rank him as one of the tougher opponents (especially in advanced/hell modes) because his quiet early-game pace tricks players into a false sense of security before the late-game sweep.

Rafayel

Rafayel plays Kitty Cards in Love and Deepspace with an impulsive, defensive, and fast-paced style that relies heavily on hoarding cards and blocking your path to victory.

Playstyle Characteristics

* Card Hoarding & Assist Cards: Rafayel tends to accumulate a large number of cards, especially saving Assist Cards to unleash near the end of the match. Players note that his card counts can drop dramatically in seconds when he deploys them.  
* Defensive & Unpredictable Moves: He actively tries to block the Player from winning, changing cup colors, demolishing kitties, and making fast moves that some players find tricky to counter.  
* Drama & Cheating: Staying true to his personality, he reacts emotionally when losing (sulking) and may even attempt to cheat by purposefully misplacing or switching cards—leading to special achievements if you catch him.

Sylus

Sylus plays a manipulative and adaptable game in Love and Deepspace Kitty Cards, often balancing between intentionally throwing the match and unleashing ruthless snowballs.

Core Strategy and Mechanics

*   Color Manipulation: Sylus tends to change the color of unmarked cups to lock you out of claiming the board, reducing your potential kitty points.  
*   The Trade Mechanic: He frequently offers card exchanges (often letting you trade two cards for one). While it seems helpful, it can swing the momentum completely.  
* Intentional Mistakes: In normal modes, players note that he occasionally makes suboptimal plays or places low-value cards in blank spaces, appearing to "throw" the match on purpose.  
* Sudden Snowballing: Despite his relaxed or helpful demeanor, if he gains an opening in advanced modes, he can aggressively sweep points, demolish your placed cats, or drag out matches.  
*   The Dual Nature:  
*   Lenient/Flirty: In normal play or when you are struggling, he may purposely make mistakes, play lower cards, or hand over good cards to let you win.  
*   Ruthless/Advanced: Once he gains an advantage, his playstyle can rapidly snowball into a aggressive lockdown where he shows no mercy.
