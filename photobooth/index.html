Mechanics

  • Limited vs Standard: Limited banners have exclusive rate-ups while standard has the base 5-star pool.

  • Ranking Memories Up: Pulling duplicates increases the rank of a memory.
  • Arcade Minigame: Compete for the highest score to earn rewards like deepspace wish and empyrean wish tickets.
