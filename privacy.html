Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection and Usage

This web application primarily operates on the client side. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal information beyond what is strictly necessary to authenticate your account if you choose to sign in.

2. Cloud & Local Storage

To save your simulator progress (such as your pull history, pity count, memory collection, and audio settings), this site uses your browser's Local Storage. If you log in via Google, X, Facebook, or Microsoft, a backup of this save data is securely stored in our Firebase database so you can sync your progress across devices.

3. Photobooth & Images

The photobooth feature processes images locally on your device. Any photos you take, upload, or edit within the photobooth are never uploaded to our servers. Everything stays on your phone or computer.

4. Third-Party Hosting

This website is hosted via Firebase Hosting. Firebase may automatically collect standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes. You can read more about this in the Google Privacy Statement.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your cloud data at any time by navigating to the Settings menu and clicking "Reset Progress", which will clear both your local and cloud data.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this project, you can reach out via Discord, Github, Instagram, and X/Twitter.
